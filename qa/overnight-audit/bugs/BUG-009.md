# BUG-009 — Caption template (look) previews do not animate (static posters only)

- **Severity:** P2 (owner-reported 2026-11-14 ~01:15 IST: "none of them preview")
- **Feature:** Studio Templates (looks) panel
- **Route:** `/projects/[id]` → Templates
- **Browser/viewport:** headless Chromium, 1440×900, local dev
- **Files:** `apps/frontend/src/features/studio/panels/LooksPanel.tsx`, `apps/frontend/src/features/studio/panels/AnimatedTemplatePreview.tsx`, `apps/frontend/src/features/studio/livePreviewBudget.ts`

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open a project in the Studio, open the Templates panel.
2. All 150 look cards show a static poster image (`/looks/<id>.webp`, e.g. "Watch this incredible trick").
3. No card ever shows a live animated preview.

## Expected
Each look card plays its animated preview (live text in the look's own font/colours) as it nears the viewport.

## Actual
- 150 preview containers render (`aspect-[8/3]` boxes), but the page holds only
  **1 canvas total** (the main stage) — zero animated look previews mount.
- Only 29/150 cards even show a poster `<img>` at a time (lazy loading); the rest
  show the swatch fallback.
- The Motion templates panel on the same page DOES show live thumbnails — the
  socials take the shared preview slots and the looks starve.

## Evidence
- `qa/overnight-audit/evidence/studio-probe4.json` — looksInitial + 4 scroll samples:
  `lookCardsWithImg: 29, canvases: 1, previewBoxes: 150` (constant across scrolling).
- `qa/overnight-audit/evidence/screenshots/looks-panel.png` — 150 of 150 looks,
  static posters only; tutorial modal open over the stage.

## Root cause (already found by the previews fix builder)
One shared 6-slot live-preview pool; Motion socials take all slots, looks starve.
Fix = per-kind split (3 slots each, ceiling 6).
