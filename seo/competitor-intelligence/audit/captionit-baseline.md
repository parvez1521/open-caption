### 2.2 What is missing or wrong (ranked)
1. **The three highest-value tool pages are invisible to Google by design [A, C — strategic].** `/transcribe`, `/motion`, `/ai-clipper` (plus `/clipper`, `/analyzer`, `/prompter`, `/create`, `/analytics`, `/brand`, `/library`) are live 200 but carry `noindex, nofollow` and are excluded from the sitemap. These are the natural search landing pages ("transcribe video free", "AI clipper", "motion graphics templates") and the AdSense inventory. The sitemap "gap" is deliberate, not a bug — it is a product decision that has not been made. Do not add them to the sitemap while noindex remains.
2. **Every canonical / og:url / sitemap `<loc>` / robots `Host:` points at the apex `https://captionit.in`, which 308-redirects to `https://www.captionit.in/` [A, re-verified NEW].** Live: `curl -sI https://captionit.in/` → `308 Permanent Redirect`, `Location: https://www.captionit.in/`. Fix: canonicalise to `www` everywhere (or flip the redirect); the code fallback `PRODUCTION_URL = "https://open-caption-ten.vercel.app"` (`lib/seo.ts:19` at main) makes this env-dependent — live correctness today rests on `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` being set in Vercel.
3. **`/onboarding` is accidentally indexable and duplicates the homepage exactly [A, C — confirmed at main NEW].** Live 200, no robots meta, no canonical; title/description byte-identical to the homepage (verified by me live 2026-11-13 ~22:50 IST).
