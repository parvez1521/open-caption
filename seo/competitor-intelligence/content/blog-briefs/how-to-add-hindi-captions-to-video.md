- **Intent:** informational (how-to), India-first.
- **Parent:** `/add-captions-to-video` (guide parent). Tool parent:

- **Why this guide:** the only guide-format result in the India top 5 for
  "add captions to video" is VideoCaptions.ai's Hindi guide — a proven
  slot with one incumbent. Captionit's Hindi/Hinglish support (Roman
  script Hinglish via a fine-tuned Whisper model; Devanagari Hindi as a

- **Outline:** (1) Hindi vs Hinglish — which script your audience reads
  (Roman Hinglish for Reels/Shorts, Devanagari for YouTube/long-form);
  (2) transcribe in Hindi/Hinglish in the browser; (3) fix Roman-script
  spellings the model gets wrong; (4) pick a look that reads well in
  Roman and Devanagari; (5) export MP4 for Reels/Shorts or SRT/VTT
  (Devanagari SRT) for YouTube.
- **Product facts (verify against `audit/captionit-baseline.md` before
  writing):** 14 languages; Hinglish = Oriserve Whisper-Hindi2Hinglish-
