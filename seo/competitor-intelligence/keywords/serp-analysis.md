# SERP Analysis — Captionit.in keyword research

- **Date of all observations:** 2026-11-13
- **Device:** desktop (browser.search returns desktop search results; no mobile SERP was observed)
- **Source:** browser.search (text queries only; no live browser, no Google account, no geo-targeted SERP)
- **Market method:** each seed was run twice — once as a plain global-English query, once with an India angle (added "India", "free", "Hindi"/"Hinglish", or "Reels" phrasing). The India pass is a *query-phrasing proxy*, not a geo-targeted google.co.in SERP — browser.search exposes no location parameter, so India results reflect what the query wording surfaces, not a true Indian user's SERP.
- **Result depth:** browser.search returned ~5 results per query. Positions beyond the top ~5 were NOT observed. No ranking positions beyond "appeared in the returned top 5" are claimed.
- **Metrics:** search volume, keyword difficulty, traffic and exact ranking positions are UNMEASURED for every keyword (no paid provider available; DataForSEO not approved). All competition/value judgements below are qualitative, labelled as such.
- **Competitor check:** captions.ai, autocaption.io and opus.pro were checked with three additional brand searches on 2026-11-13 (see §13).

## 14. Limitations — read before using this file

1. **No volume / difficulty / traffic data.** Every volume and difficulty value is UNMEASURED. No paid provider was used. Do not quote search volumes or KD scores from this research.
2. **Top ~5 only.** browser.search returned about 5 results per query. Positions 6–10, People Also Ask, video results and AI overviews were not observed.
3. **India pass is a phrasing proxy.** No geo-targeted SERP was available. A real google.co.in / Indian-user SERP may differ.
4. **Desktop only.** Mobile SERPs (where most Reels creators search) were not observed.
5. **Single-day snapshot.** All observations are 2026-11-13. SERPs move; listicles in this set were updated within days of the research date.
6. **"Not observed" ≠ "does not rank".** A competitor absent from a top-5 snapshot may rank at position 6+, on a different query variant, or on mobile.
7. **Vendor claims are as quoted in SERP snippets** (e.g. accuracy %, style counts, prices). They are the competitors' own claims as surfaced by search, not independently verified facts.
